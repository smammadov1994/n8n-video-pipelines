# First clip: how AI models work

About 45 seconds spoken (roughly 120 words). Plain talking to camera, no walking.

## Spoken words

You have probably used an AI chat model. Here is what it actually is.

It is a giant guessing machine. It was shown a huge amount of writing, and each time it had to guess the next word. Guess wrong, and its settings get nudged a tiny bit. Do that billions of times, and the guesses get very good.

Those settings are just numbers. Billions of them. Nobody typed them in. They were learned.

So when you ask a question, it does not look up an answer. It writes one, a word at a time, each word the most fitting next step.

That is why it can sound sure and still be wrong. It is predicting, not remembering.

Once you know that, you use it better.
