Mango is a very round, chubby cartoon cat shaped like a soft pear: small head blending into a big wide belly, short stubby arms and legs, tiny rounded paws with no claws. Plain apricot-orange fur with NO stripes and no markings, a cream-white oval belly patch and cream-white muzzle, a small pink heart-shaped nose, big round leaf-green eyes with large black pupils, two small rounded triangular ears where the left ear tip is folded down, three short white whiskers on each cheek, one short thick tail with a cream-white tip. Mango wears a teal-turquoise bib apron with one big sunny-yellow round pocket on the front, and a soft floppy teal-turquoise chef hat with a sunny-yellow band, tilted slightly to one side.
